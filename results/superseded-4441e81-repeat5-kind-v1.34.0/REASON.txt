Superseded session, build 0.1.0-9-g4441e81. NOT part of the recorded matrix.

Arms A-D completed here (20 trials). Arm E aborted at repeat 3 when six of ten
TCP flows died during the steady-state hold, before the trigger was issued. The
harness refused to measure through it, which was correct.

The cause was a defect in drainwatch, not in the cluster:

  The service-endpoint-ready preflight check counted ANY ready endpoint, and
  endpoints were never removed from the readiness map when they left the
  EndpointSlice. The previous trial's endpoint could therefore satisfy this
  trial's check - which is why it passed in 0 ms - letting flows be dialed
  before this trial's pod endpoint had been programmed into kube-proxy on the
  node. When kube-proxy converged it flushed conntrack for the NodePort and
  tore down the subset of flows established in that window.

Arm E is the arm where this was most likely to bite, because it is the only one
whose previous trial ends by scaling to zero, giving a 1 -> 0 -> 1 endpoint
transition between trials.

The check is now scoped to this trial's pod and requires the endpoint to have
been continuously ready for --endpoint-stable-ms (default 2000) before flows are
dialed. Because that changes the measuring instrument, the whole matrix was
rerun from a single frozen build rather than stitching a fixed arm E onto four
arms recorded with the defect present. These 20 trials are kept for the record
and are used for no number anywhere.
